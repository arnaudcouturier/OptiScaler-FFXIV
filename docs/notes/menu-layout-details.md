# Menu layout: rules, controls and tests in full

Material moved out of [FEATURES.md](../FEATURES.md#menu-layout) on 2026-10-23; the paragraphs are copied from it with only internal names renamed or removed and the in-game status corrections of 2026-10-23.

The in-game menu keeps upstream's tabs and cards; the fork's cards join them through the registry in `ext/core/MenuRegistry.h`. Four rules keep the result predictable. **FFXIV tab.** Everything that needs the FFXIV build match sits in one tab, "FFXIV", third in the tab bar right after Frame Gen, and the tab is hidden in every other game. Its cards, in order: Window mode, FFXIV HDR10 output, Game jitter, Character preview, HUD-less (UI-free frame generation), FFXIV Companion (nameplates), HUD-less diagnostics, Advanced (FFXIV) (a closed fold with `[Hotfix] RenderSizeFromWidthHeight` and `[XeFG] ResetOnUpscalerChange`, both FFXIV only). The cards keep their ids, so the pins of the Custom Tab still work. **Draw once.** A group of upstream cards that share one drawing function (the Frame Gen runtime cards, Mipmap Bias with Anisotropic Filtering, the advanced Compatibility cards) is drawn once per tab, at the place of its first card. Before, a fork card between two cards of such a group made the whole group show twice. So a fork card sits before or after such a group, never inside it: Automatic Mipmap Bias comes after Anisotropic Filtering, and the two DLSS-G cards, Frame generation unlocks and then Automatic multi frame generation, after the last runtime card; Forced quality level (was "Quality Preset") follows Upscale Ratio Override. **Layout table.** `ext/core/MenuLayout.h` says where each fork card goes (the card it follows, per card id); the order no longer depends on the link order of the source files, and a card without an entry goes to the end of its tab. The Custom Tab draws its pinned cards in this same order, so a card that moved shows at its new place (the pins themselves are by card id and still work). **Manifest.** `ext/core/MenuManifest.h` lists the FFXIV-only cards, every released card id (retired ids get an alias), and the config keys that stay ini-only on purpose; `tests/ext/menu/menu_manifest.cpp` checks all of it against the sources offline, including that every card has a title equal to its picker name, that every tab is one run of the menu order, and that an FFXIV-only card tests the game build in its own visible predicate. Diagnostics (HUD-less diagnostics, the ReShade add-on probe, the Companion diagnostics) are closed by default. Code: `ext/core/` (`SourceTracker.h`, `MenuLayout.h`, `MenuManifest.h`, `MenuRegistry.*`). Seams: menu `SourceTracker::Claim` (the tab loop) and `FfxivTabVisible` (one `tabs[]` row). Status: offline tests only; the new layout has not been looked at in the game yet.

**Controls for the less common keys.** Every config key the fork binds has a control in the card it belongs to, or is ini-only on purpose (`[HudLess] Diagnostics` and `ReShadeProbe`, listed in the manifest and marked "No menu control" in `HudLess.ini`); `tests/ext/menu/menu_key_controls.cpp` checks that offline, `controls_text.cpp` pins each new widget, and `ini_fragments.cpp` checks that every bound key has a commented line in its ini fragment (the new `Companion.ini` documents `[Companion] HudReplacement` and `HudInterpolation`).
